Tulip Double Surprise

It has exterior lower petals of feathered pale to deep magenta-pink that blend to bright white in the center of its many petticoat-petal layers. Tulip Class: Peony Flowering (Double Late). Bulb size: 12 cm/up. Full to partial sunlight. Bloom time in horticultural zone 5: Late April. Plant 6” to 8” deep and 6” apart. HZ: 3-7. Height: 16”.
